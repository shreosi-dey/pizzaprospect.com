Our Philosophy

Pizza is an
Art Form.

At Pizza Prospect, we believe every great pizza begins long before it touches the flame. It starts with intention — selecting the finest semolina flour, cultivating our own slow-fermented sourdough starter, and sourcing only the richest produce from trusted farms.

Our woodfired oven reaches 450°C — not a degree less. In 90 seconds of fierce, honest heat, the crust blisters and chars at the edges while the centre remains pillowy soft. This is not fast food. This is a craft we've spent years perfecting.

"We don't make pizza for everyone. We make it for those who can taste the difference."

Born for Hinjewadi — Pune's most discerning food crowd. For those who've had the real thing and refuse to settle for less.

Our Promise

Pure Vegetarian.
Always.

Pizza Prospect is a proudly 100% vegetarian kitchen — not out of obligation but out of belief that plant-forward cooking, executed with skill and premium ingredients, is among the most exciting food in the world.

Our kitchen has never served, stored, or touched non-vegetarian ingredients. Every surface, utensil, and ingredient is certified vegetarian. This is a promise we keep with every single pizza.

For our Jain guests — a dedicated Jain menu with no root vegetables, no onion, no garlic, yet zero compromise on flavour. Same woodfired craft. Same premium experience.
